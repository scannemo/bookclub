Meeting time

We meet the first Thursday of every odd-numbered month, at 7pm at a location that strikes us as good (see the chat). We try to avoid changing/revisiting the time more than once a year, since there will always be conflicts, and it's exhausting to make low-stakes decisions among 20ish people too often. On even-numbered months there is also a suggested "hang with people who like books" on the first Thursday. It's good to read the book, but if you don't, still come! Come for the safe spaces and dope feminine energy.
